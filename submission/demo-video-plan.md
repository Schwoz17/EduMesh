# EduMesh AI — Demo Video Plan (about 2½ minutes)

The goal is one thing the judges can't doubt: **EduMesh answers real questions with the network switched off.**

## Before recording

- [ ] An Android phone with Chrome, battery above 50%, Do Not Disturb on.
- [ ] Open the live link on Wi-Fi. Tap **Start learning**, then **Setup → Download for offline** (use the "Lite" model on older phones). Wait until it shows "✅ Saved on this phone".
- [ ] Test once in airplane mode: ask "What is photosynthesis?" and confirm it answers. If Phone AI is too slow on this phone, set **Setup → Textbook only** for the recording and say so.
- [ ] Don't clear the site's data after downloading: that also deletes the AI model. The start-up screen only shows on the first visit, so if you want it in the video, record it on a second phone (or a fresh Chrome profile) before anything else.
- [ ] Prepare the Agricultural Science lesson-pack file on a second phone (`examples/agric-pack.edumesh.json` from the repo), ready to send by Xender or Bluetooth.
- [ ] Record the phone screen (Android screen recorder with sound on), plus an optional second camera on the phone's status bar for the airplane icon.

## Shot list

| # | Time | On screen | Voice-over |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 0:00–0:10 | Phone, then pull down quick settings and turn on **airplane mode**. Hold on the icon. | "This phone has no internet. No Wi-Fi, no data." |
| 2 | 0:10–0:25 | Open EduMesh from the home screen. The welcome screen shows "Wetin you wan learn today?" (Optional: cut in the start-up screen recorded on a second phone.) | "This is EduMesh AI, a free tutor that works completely offline." |
| 3 | 0:25–0:45 | Tap **"Abeg explain Ohm's law"**. The answer appears in Pidgin; zoom in on the **📖 Physics — Electric Current and Ohm's Law** source. | "I ask in Pidgin, it answers in Pidgin, and it shows the textbook page it used." |
| 4 | 0:45–1:00 | Switch the language to **Yoruba mix**. Ask "What is photosynthesis?" and show the answer. | "Five language modes: English, Pidgin, Yoruba, Hausa and Igbo." |
| 5 | 1:00–1:25 | **Progress** tab → **Take a 5-question quiz**. Answer two questions (one wrong on purpose) and show the green/red feedback. Finish, then show the gauge and weak topic. | "Quizzes predict my next score and flag the topics I keep missing." |
| 6 | 1:25–1:50 | Open **Aa → Voice Mode**. The screen switches to high contrast. Ask a question; the answer is **read aloud** (let 5 seconds of audio play). | "For blind students, Voice Mode reads every answer aloud. On first launch, a double-tap anywhere turns it on." |
| 7 | 1:50–2:10 | **Setup → Import pack file.** Receive the Agricultural Science pack by Xender or Bluetooth and import it. Ask "What is loamy soil?" and show the answer. | "Teachers share new lessons phone to phone. Still no data." |
| 8 | 2:10–2:25 | Pull down quick settings again: **airplane mode is still on**. End card: the EduMesh logo, the live link and the GitHub link. | "Everything you saw ran with zero internet. EduMesh AI: a tutor for every student, for zero naira of data." |

## Tips

- Keep each shot short and steady. Cut waiting time in editing, but **never cut the airplane-mode icon** out of shots 1 and 8. That's your proof.
- Add captions; judges often watch without sound.
- If anything fails while recording, show Textbook Mode and keep going. It is part of the design, not a failure.
